The Northern Territory rules

  • Registration is needed for building work worth $12,000 or more, including new homes, extensions and commercial buildings.
  • Renovations that don't add floor area or change the structure, freestanding sheds and fences don't need registration.
  • Residential and commercial registrations come in restricted (lower height) and unrestricted (any height) categories.
  • You need a Cert IV in Building, 3 years' experience, 3 references from registered practitioners and $50,000 in net tangible assets.
  • A company needs a registered director or nominee who supervises the work, and $50,000 in net assets.

Official regulator: Building Practitioners Board. Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

Your steps to a Northern Territory licence

  1. Work out your category: residential or commercial, restricted or unrestricted.
  2. Gather your Cert IV, experience résumé and project list, 3 references and a net assets certificate from your accountant.
  3. Apply to the Building Practitioners Board, with us checking every document first.

Northern Territory licence questions

Who regulates builders in the NT?

The Building Practitioners Board, under the NT Building Act.

Do I need a licence for every job in the NT?

No. Registration is needed for building work worth $12,000 or more, like new homes and extensions. Renovations that don't add floor area or change the structure, freestanding sheds and fences don't need it. Splitting a job into smaller contracts to stay under the limit can lead to action.

What do I need to register as an NT builder?

A Cert IV in Building, 3 years' relevant experience, 3 references from registered practitioners, and a certificate from your accountant showing $50,000 in net tangible assets.

How much does an NT licence cost?

Dan's fees start from $2,000 for a general builder licence, ex GST and government fees.

Can I use an interstate licence in the NT?

Usually, yes. Mutual recognition can let a current licence from another state or New Zealand get you the equivalent licence in the NT, as long as it covers the same work. Dan checks the match before you apply.
