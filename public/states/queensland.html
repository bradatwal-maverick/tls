The Queensland rules

  • Licence needed for building work over $3,300 (hydraulic services design over $1,100).
  • Licence needed at any value for building design, chemical termite management, completed residential building inspections, fire protection, gas fitting, mechanical services, plumbing and drainage, and site classification.
  • Your company must hold its own QBCC licence. Trading as a company under your personal licence is a breach (to verify with Dan).
  • Unlicensed work can void the owner's Queensland Home Warranty Insurance.

Official regulator: Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC). Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

Your steps to a Queensland licence

Queensland licence questions

Do I need a QBCC licence for a small job?

Building work over $3,300 needs a licence. Some trades need one at any value, including plumbing, gas fitting and fire protection.

What QBCC licence do I need?

It depends on your trade and the work. The common builder pathway is builder low rise. Dan will confirm your class before you apply.

How much does a QBCC licence application cost?

Dan's service starts from $2,000 for a general builder licence, ex GST and QBCC fees. Trade licences start from $495.

Does my company need its own QBCC licence?

Yes. The company entity needs its own QBCC licence (to verify with Dan).

Licensed interstate?

Interstate licence holders: check mutual recognition with the QBCC before applying. Official information.
