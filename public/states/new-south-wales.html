The New South Wales rules

  • Contractor licence needed for residential building or trade work over $5,000, labour and materials and GST included.
  • Licence types: individual contractor licence, company or partnership contractor licence, and qualified supervisor certificate.
  • Licences run for 1, 3 or 5 years.
  • A National Police Certificate, no older than 4 months, is needed for individuals.
  • Apprentices can't be licensed until their apprenticeship is finished.
  • Your company must hold its own licence. Your personal licence doesn't cover it (to verify with Dan).

Official regulator: Building Commission NSW. Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

New South Wales licence questions

Do I need a licence for work under $5,000 in NSW?

Work valued at $5,000 or less, including labour and materials and GST, is generally minor work that doesn't need a contractor licence. Check the NSW minor trade work guidance for your job.

Who regulates builder licences in NSW?

Building Commission NSW regulates contractor licences. Applications are lodged through Service NSW. Many people still search for NSW Fair Trading, the older name.

How much does an NSW builder licence cost?

Dan's general builder licence service starts from $2,000, ex GST and government fees. Pricing depends on your situation.

What if my company needs a licence?

A company or partnership doing the work needs its own contractor licence, with a qualified supervisor. We map who signs what before you apply.
