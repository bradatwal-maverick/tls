The South Australia rules

  • No dollar threshold: anyone who carries out or organises building work for others needs a building work contractor's licence.
  • All building work must be supervised by a registered building work supervisor. CBS checks supervisor experience with a work history record and a technical interview.
  • At least $10,000 in net assets for specified work, like carpentry or bricklaying. A restricted licence exists for subcontractors who don't meet this.
  • A National Police Certificate, issued within the last 12 months, is needed.
  • Tell CBS within 14 days if your supervisor or directors change.

Official regulator: Consumer and Business Services (CBS). Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

South Australia licence questions

Is there a dollar threshold for a SA builder licence?

No. In South Australia anyone who carries out or organises building work for others needs a licence, with no dollar threshold.

Do I need $10,000 in net assets?

For specified building work, like carpentry or bricklaying, yes. If you don't have it, you can apply for a restricted licence as a subcontractor.

Does my company need a supervisor?

Yes. A company must nominate a registered building work supervisor when it applies, and tell CBS within 14 days of any change.

Who regulates builders in South Australia?

Consumer and Business Services (CBS) issues building work contractor licences.

How much does a SA licence cost?

Licensed interstate?

Licensed in another state or New Zealand? Mutual recognition can let you get the equivalent licence in South Australia, if your licence is current and covers the same work. Dan checks the match before you apply. Official information.
