The Victoria rules

  • Registration needed for domestic building work over $10,000 (a major domestic building contract).
  • Domestic building insurance needed for work of $16,000 or more.
  • Classes: Domestic Builder (Unlimited) and Domestic Builder (Limited), for example carpentry or glazing.
  • The BPC replaced the VBA on 1 July 2025. Recent changes, including an online exam from January 2026, are to verify on the BPC site.

Official regulator: Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC). Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

Victoria licence questions

Is the VBA still the regulator in Victoria?

No. The Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC) replaced the Victorian Building Authority (VBA) on 1 July 2025. Both names are still searched.

When do I need registration in Victoria?

Domestic building work over $10,000 needs registration. Domestic building insurance is needed at $16,000 or more.

Can I use my licence from another state in Victoria?

Some licensed practitioners can use automatic mutual recognition. Check the BPC page on automatic mutual recognition for your trade.

What changed for builder registration in 2026?

Reported changes include an online exam from January 2026 (to verify on the BPC site).

Licensed interstate?

Automatic mutual recognition for Victoria: see the BPC page. Official information.
