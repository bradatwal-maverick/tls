The Western Australia rules

  • Registered building contractor needed for work of $20,000 or more that needs a building permit.
  • For Class 10a work, like sheds, garages and carports, the threshold is $50,000 (from 1 July 2026).
  • Every building contractor needs a nominated supervisor who is a registered building practitioner. The usual path is a Diploma of Building plus 7 years' experience.
  • If your nominated supervisor changes, tell the Building Services Board within 7 days.
  • Building and Energy registers builders, building surveyors, building engineers and painters. Plumbers and electricians are licensed separately.

Official regulator: Building and Energy (Department of Local Government, Industry Regulation and Safety). Rules change, so check the regulator page too.

Western Australia licence questions

When do I need to be a registered building contractor in WA?

For work of $20,000 or more that needs a building permit. For Class 10a work, such as garages, carports and sheds, the threshold is $50,000.

Who regulates WA building contractors?

Building and Energy, part of the Department of Local Government, Industry Regulation and Safety.

Do I need a licence for a garage or shed?

Only once the work is $50,000 or more and needs a building permit. The threshold went up from $20,000 on 1 July 2026.

Who can be a nominated supervisor?

A registered building practitioner. The usual path is a Diploma of Building plus 7 years' experience. A company or partnership can use a director, partner or employee who holds that registration.

How much does a WA registration cost?

Licensed interstate?

Licensed in another state or New Zealand? Mutual recognition can let you get the equivalent licence in Western Australia, if your licence is current and covers the same work. Dan checks the match before you apply. Official information.
