Questions tradies ask

Do you lodge my application for me?

No. You lodge it yourself with your state regulator. We make sure everything in it is right first, and stay with you until it's approved.

Do I need a licence for small jobs?

In NSW you need a contractor licence to contract residential work worth more than $5,000 including GST, counting labour and materials. Other states have their own thresholds, so pick your state above.

I have a licence in another state. Do I start again?

Often not. Mutual recognition lets many licence holders get the matching licence. We check your licence class lines up first.

My business is a company. Do I need a separate licence?

Usually yes. A company contracting the work needs its own licence, with a supervisor who holds the right individual licence or certificate.

I don't have a qualification yet. Can you help?

We'll point you to the right qualification for your trade, so you can come back licence-ready.
